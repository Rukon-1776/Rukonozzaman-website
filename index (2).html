01 / Background

About me

I hold a BSc and an MSc in Statistics from Jagannath University. Before joining BRAC IED in March 2025, I worked at SURCH, a survey research house, on studies funded by UNICEF, and I have been part of research teams analysing Bangladesh Demographic and Health Survey (BDHS) data.

My work covers the full survey cycle: questionnaire design, sampling, field supervision, data management in CSPro and Kobo Toolbox, statistical analysis, and reporting.
